Cookie Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how the website www.maxromeexecutivechauffeur.it (the "Website") uses cookies and similar technologies, in accordance with Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code (Legislative Decree No. 196/2003, as amended) and the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your browser and remember certain information. Similar technologies include local storage and session storage.

2. Cookies used by this Website

The Website is designed to minimise data collection. Specifically:

  • Essential / technical functionality only. The Website uses browser storage (session and local storage) solely to make the booking flow work — for example, to keep your selected route and form state while you complete a reservation. These are strictly necessary for the operation of the service you requested.
  • No profiling cookies. The Website does not use profiling cookies, advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • No analytics or marketing tools. The Website does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or any other traffic-analysis or advertising tool.

Because the Website uses only strictly necessary technical storage, no cookie consent banner is required under Article 122 of the Privacy Code, and none is displayed.

4. Managing and deleting cookies

You can control or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings at any time:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data;
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data;
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data;
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Disabling browser storage may affect the booking form, which relies on it to remember your selection while you complete the reservation.

For questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at maxromeexecutivechauffeur@outlook.it. For information on how we process personal data, see our Privacy Policy.
